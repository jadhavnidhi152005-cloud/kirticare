Two-Access Campus Redressal Architecture

Report, Track & Solve Campus Infrastructure Issues

A dual-access system designed with clear separation: Student Access to register complaints under verified student profiles and track real-time resolution, and College Authority Access handed to college administration & maintenance staff to inspect, solve, and resolve problems.

Select Your Access Portal

CampusCare separates user workflows into two dedicated access portals based on your campus role

Access 1 • Student Portal

Student Grievance & Tracking Desk

For all enrolled students to register infrastructure problems and monitor resolution status in real-time.

  • Lodge Grievances: Submit issues for Classroom, Washroom, Wi-Fi, Electricity, or Water.
  • Student Identity Tag: Complaints are permanently registered under your verified student name & roll number.
  • Live Tracking: Follow tickets across 4 clear stages (Logged → Under Review → In Progress → Resolved).
  • Technician Visibility: View administrative remarks and technician dispatch updates.

Access 2 • College Authority

College Resolution & Staff Desk

Handed over exclusively to College Administration, Facility Supervisors & Technicians to inspect, solve, and resolve problems.

  • Restricted Staff Desk: Protected administrative portal requiring authorized staff sign in.
  • Filter & Review: View campus grievances filtered by category, urgency, and student.
  • Solve & Update: Change complaint status to Pending, In Progress, Resolved, or Rejected.
  • Technician Remarks: Record resolution notes and work logs directly on each ticket.

How Grievance Redressal Works

1. Submit Ticket

Select category, room location, and urgency. Receive a unique tracking ID instantly.

2. Technician Assigned

Campus authorities review and dispatch electricians, plumbers, or IT technicians immediately.

3. Problem Resolved

Track status in real-time. Administration records resolution notes upon successful fix.
